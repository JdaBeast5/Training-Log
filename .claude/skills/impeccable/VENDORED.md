  sessions; the skill's own fallback applies — read `reference/craft-floor.md`
  plus the command reference (`distill`, `layout`, `typeset`, `polish`) and
  verify in Chromium by hand.
- Owner-agreed exceptions that override the craft floor here: the glass & glow
  look (concept A, chosen by the owner), the Up next session ring, and the
  day-hue gradient title. "The brief wins" (SKILL.md).
