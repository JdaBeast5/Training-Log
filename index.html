💡 Keep In Mind
Protein first. Hit 230-250g before worrying about anything else — this is what protects muscle in the deficit.
Zone 2 cardio, 2-3x/week. 20-30 min incline walk or bike. Don't turn it into HIIT — that eats into lifting recovery.
8-10k steps/day. Your biggest fat-loss lever that doesn't cost you strength.
Progressive overload. Hit the top of a rep range on all sets → add weight next session.
Sleep 7-9 hrs. Non-negotiable for recomp — this is when muscle actually rebuilds.
Weigh in weekly, not daily. Track the trend over 2-3 weeks, not day-to-day noise.
Order doesn't matter, consistency does. Shift days around your week freely — just try to land on a rest day after 2 straight lifting days when you can.
